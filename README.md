## Overview

**Queue Buddy** is a cross-platform mobile application that digitizes and intelligently manages customer queues in bank branches.

What makes it distinctive: customers can interact with the system **in Bambara** — the most widely spoken language in Mali — removing the language barrier that excludes a large portion of the population from standard French-only interfaces.

Built with Flutter for cross-platform delivery and integrated with a Bambara ASR model (RobotsMali / Soloba) for voice interaction.

---

## Key Features

- 🎙️ **Bambara Voice Interface** — customers speak in Bambara to join a queue or request a service
- 🎫 **Digital Ticketing** — join queues remotely and receive a digital ticket
- 🔔 **Real-time Notifications** — alerts when your turn is approaching
- 📊 **Staff Dashboard** — queue analytics and flow management for bank staff
- 📱 **Cross-Platform** — iOS and Android from a single Flutter codebase

---

## Context

In Mali, a significant portion of bank clients communicate primarily in Bambara rather than French. Existing queue management systems — when they exist at all — operate entirely in French, creating friction and exclusion.

Queue Buddy addresses this directly by integrating a Bambara speech recognition model into the queue workflow, making financial services more accessible.
